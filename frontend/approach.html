Disease Grading · ETDRS Scale

5 Stages of Diabetic Retinopathy

Our model classifies each retinal image into one of these internationally recognised severity grades.

🟢

Stage 0

No DR — normal retinal vasculature, no lesions detected.

No Risk
🟡

Stage 1 · Mild

Microaneurysms only — tiny balloon-like vessel swellings.

Low Risk
🟠

Stage 2 · Moderate

Haemorrhages, exudates, cotton-wool spots visible.

Moderate
🔴

Stage 3 · Severe

Many blocked vessels, early new vessel growth triggered.

High Risk
⛔

Stage 4 · PDR

Proliferative DR — vitreous bleed, retinal detachment risk.

Critical
