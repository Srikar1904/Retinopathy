Training configuration

How the model was trained.

The training pipeline combines transfer learning, image augmentation, class weighting and selective fine-tuning to address the imbalanced DR classes.

🧠

Transfer Learning

ResNet-50 was initialized with ImageNet pretrained weights and adapted for retinal image classification.

🖼️

Image Augmentation

Training images used random horizontal flipping, small rotations and zoom augmentation to improve robustness.

⚖️

Class Weighting

Higher weights were assigned to underrepresented severity classes so that rare stages contributed more strongly during training.

🔧

Fine-Tuning

The final 30 ResNet layers were fine-tuned while Batch Normalization layers remained frozen.

📐

Image Size

Every input image is resized to 224 × 224 pixels before being passed through the ResNet-50 preprocessing pipeline.

🎯

Five-Class Output

The final Softmax layer produces scores for No DR, Mild, Moderate, Severe and Proliferative DR.

Responsible AI

What the model doesn't tell you.

Reporting limitations clearly is an important part of evaluating an AI-assisted medical screening prototype.

Class Imbalance

The APTOS dataset contains substantially more No DR images than Severe and Proliferative cases. This contributes to differences in per-class performance.

Lower Rare-Class Performance

Severe and Proliferative DR have lower precision, recall and F1 scores than the No DR and Moderate categories in the held-out test results.

Confidence Is Not Probability

The displayed confidence comes from the model's Softmax output. It should not be interpreted as a calibrated medical probability.

Grad-CAM Is Explanatory

Grad-CAM indicates image regions associated with the model prediction. It does not constitute definitive lesion detection or a medical explanation.

Important: RetinaCare AI is an AI-assisted screening and research prototype. Its predictions should not replace examination or diagnosis by a qualified healthcare professional.
