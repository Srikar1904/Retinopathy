About Diabetic Retinopathy

Why the eye
reveals so much

Diabetic Retinopathy is damage to retinal blood vessels caused by high blood sugar — the leading cause of preventable blindness worldwide.

👁️

100M+ people affected

DR impacts over 100 million diabetics globally. India alone has 77 million diabetic patients.

⚡

Early detection prevents 90% of vision loss

Caught early, most DR-related blindness is entirely preventable with timely treatment.

🏥

Critical shortage of specialists

Only 1 ophthalmologist per 100,000 patients in rural India — AI bridges this gap.

RetinaCare AI

AI-powered early detection of Diabetic Retinopathy and multi-eye disease screening — built to make specialist-grade care accessible to every patient, everywhere.
